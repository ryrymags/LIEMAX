Reading the model. Camera FOV is held constant across configurations, so the screen's real size and your distance determine how much of the frame it fills — relative scale is accurate, absolute degrees on your monitor are not. The lit footprint is sized off the physical screen first, then the native 1.43:1 frame is cropped (never stretched) to fill it; black bars are unlit screen, the lighter gray is the auditorium itself. Per IMAX classic-design geometry, all seating sits within roughly one screen height of the screen, which reaches near floor level. The amber block at the screen base is a simple average-height (5 ft 9 in) scale reference, not an anatomical figure, toggle it on or off per view. Riser height per row is held constant as a simplification.
